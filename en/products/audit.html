01 / AI Workflow Audit

Find the part of your week
AI should take over.

30–60 min · one-page report · 3 priority scenarios · 1 usable template

Enterprise case service · by consultation

Not everything should go to AI, and not everything should stay with you. The audit first separates the two.

In one 30–60 minute interview about your real work, we'll go through these steps:

  1. Describe real scenarios: no preparation needed — just talk about work that keeps repeating this week.
  2. Classify the nature: repetitive transfer, rule-based judgment, or decisions that must stay with people.
  3. Mark the risk points: what can be automated, where human review must remain, and how to desensitize data.
  4. Define next steps: 3 priority scenarios + 1 template you can pilot immediately.

7 days later we do a follow-up review: did it work, where did it stall, and should we continue.

The audit is only the first step. Get one repetitive task running, then decide whether to scale.

Enterprise case service, arranged by consultation. You don't need to upload complete sensitive material — talk process first, then tools.
