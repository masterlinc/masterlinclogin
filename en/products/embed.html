03 / Organization AI Embedding

Embed AI into a department's
highest-repetition processes.

4–6 weeks · 5–10 AI workflows · SOP · training · ROI report

Enterprise case service · by consultation

People who do this usually fall into two camps: tech teams can build workflows but don't understand management; management consultants understand organizations but AI stays at the PPT level. Our position is having both.

Six weeks, at this pace:

  1. Week 1 · Full audit: audit every member of the target department, collect work records, process documents and SOPs, produce a department efficiency audit report.
  2. Week 2–3 · Process redesign: pick the TOP 5–10 high-repetition, high-AI-fit processes and design AI alternatives for each — not "use AI to write", but embed AI into the process node.
  3. Week 4–5 · Build & train: build working AI workflows (GPTs / Dify / Feishu bot / WeCom bot, etc.), train the whole department, deliver a manual and training videos.
  4. Week 6 · Track results: compare time spent before and after, generate an ROI report, deliver and move into continuous optimization.
AI tools change every month. What organizations need is not "buying tools" but an outside eye that keeps scanning: what new capability could replace which of your processes.

Deliverables: 5–10 AI workflows + operation SOP + internal training videos + a tracking dashboard.
