02 / Team AI Workshop

Help a team find
its AI entry points.

Half-day 3 hours · full-day 6 hours · in-person or online

This is not AI training — it's management diagnosis. What leaders buy is not a tech course, but efficiency gains and visible outcomes.

Three hours, three questions solved:

  1. Hour 1 · Awaken (collective audit): everyone lists last week's work, classifies each item, and gets their own "fake management ratio". Small-group share: which number surprised you most?
  2. Hour 2 · See (team heatmap): aggregate everyone's classification onto one wall, find the team's "collective fake management zone". Vote: if we could change one thing first, what?
  3. Hour 3 · Build (first AI workflow): pick the top consensus pain point, demo AI cutting that process from 30 minutes to 3, then each person runs it with their own real data.

Deliverables: team efficiency heatmap + TOP3 automation opportunities + the first working team AI workflow with an SOP.

It naturally leads to the next step — at the end everyone asks: what about the other processes?

Best for: 10–30 person teams (half-day), or 30–80 person departments (full-day). Email first to discuss needs, then decide the version.
