AI Meeting Notes: The Real Danger Is Not Missing Words

Field note from management · 2026-08

AI turning an hour of audio into one page of notes is no longer hard.

The real danger: it can turn "someone mentioned" into "the team decided", and pad a vague remark into what looks like a complete action item.

After 20 years in management, what worries me most is not ugly notes — it's the three disputes that show up after a meeting:

  • Did we actually decide this or not?
  • Who agreed to own it?
  • Who set the deadline?

So my AI notes keep only four columns:

  1. Confirmed decisions;
  2. Pending items to verify;
  3. Owner and deadline;
  4. The verbatim evidence.

If the recording has no owner or date, mark it "pending" — don't let AI guess.

AI can organize facts. Accountability must stay with people.

What causes more rework in your meetings: missing action items, or disputes over who agreed to what?
