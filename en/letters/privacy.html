You Can Judge Whether to Use AI Without Handing Over Company Files

Field note from management · 2026-08

People ask me: management logs and business data are confidential — how can I give them to an external AI?

That concern is correct.

To judge whether a management task suits AI, the first step doesn't need raw files. Just describe the last real scenario out loud:

  • Where does the input come from?
  • Who makes the judgment in between?
  • What is the final deliverable?
  • Who reviews it?
  • Which step most often causes rework?

With just these five things, you can usually classify it: repetitive transfer, rule-based judgment, experience-based judgment, or something that should not be automated at all.

If you do pilot it, use only a small desensitized sample — replace names, client names, amounts and account identifiers. Material stays in the environment you control; AI only handles the minimum necessary.

Judge the process first, then decide on the tool. Don't hand over the whole company just to try AI.

Without showing any files, can you explain your last rework process in these five steps?
